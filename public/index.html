How a project runs

Four stages, agreed in writing, with a working preview you can open throughout.

  1. Discover

    We learn how your business works, what has to change and what success looks like, then agree scope, timeline and cost in writing.

  2. Design

    Flows, screens and architecture are designed and reviewed with you before any production code is written.

  3. Build

    We build in short cycles with a working preview you can open at any time, and test on real devices and real data.

  4. Launch and support

    We release, monitor and maintain. You own the code, the accounts and the documentation.
