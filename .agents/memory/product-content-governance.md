---
name: Product content governance
description: Rules for publishing Habinix-owned product information safely and consistently.
---

Habinix products must remain clearly distinct from Habinix client services. Only officially supplied product facts, assets, features, store URLs, support information, and legal copy may be published.

**Why:** Product pages need to stay app-store-ready without creating fictional product claims, support details, launch information, or links.

**How to apply:** Keep product entries modular and optional. When official information is absent, use clear non-link placeholders and omit detail sections rather than filling gaps with marketing content. Maintain separate product-level support, privacy, and terms paths in addition to company-level pages.
